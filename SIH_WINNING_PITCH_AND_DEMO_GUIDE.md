## 🎯 7 Trap Questions SIH Judges Ask (And How to Answer Them)

### Q1: *"Strava, Cult.fit, ya HealthifyMe se aapka solution kaise superior hai?"*
- **Winning Answer**: 
  > *"Sir, Strava is purely an activity tracker (it records what you did, but doesn't schedule your day). Cult.fit offers pre-recorded video classes with fixed durations. HealthifyMe counts calories. 
  > **VYRA solves the fundamental reason people quit**: time mismatch. None of those apps read your timetable or scale a 45-minute workout to a 15-minute routine when your day is packed. VYRA is not just a tracker, it is an adaptive time-intelligence engine."*

### Q2: *"Agar user internet connection na ho, toh kya app fail ho jayegi?"*
- **Winning Answer**: 
  > *"Bilkul nahi sir. VYRA ko offline-first design kiya gaya hai. Local SharedPreferences and offline databases ensure karte hain ki exercise logging, timers, barcode lookups, aur emergency SOS alerts bina internet ke bhi flawlessly execute hon aur internet reconnect hone par zero-loss delta sync karein."*

### Q3: *"Aap lab report analysis me AI use kar rahe hain. Agar AI ne galat dawai ya advice de di toh legal liability kiska hoga?"*
- **Winning Answer**: 
  > *"Sir, humne strict architectural guardrails lagaye hain:
  > 1. VYRA never prescribes medications or diagnoses diseases.
  > 2. Code level par critical biological markers (e.g. extreme glucose, kidney creatinine) par diet advice completely short-circuit ho jaati hai.
  > 3. Hum sirf everyday Indian dietary foods (like palak, dal) suggest karte hain with mandatory medical disclaimers."*

### Q4: *"Coins and Rewards system ko log fake activities log karke exploit kar sakte hain?"*
- **Winning Answer**: 
  > *"Humne double-spending aur gaming prevention ke liye mathematical idempotency aur strict daily earn caps implement kiye hain. Ek din me maximum limit ke baad coins earn nahi kiye ja sakte, aur speed/GPS plausibility checks unrealistic spikes ko reject karte hain."*

### Q5: *"Theme switch karne se battery par kya asar padta hai?"*
- **Winning Answer**: 
  > *"Humne pure OLED-optimized Obsidian Dark (`#0F131D`) provide kiya hai jo pixels ko turn off karke battery consumption 30-40% tak reduce karta hai, aur high-glare daytime outdoor workouts ke liye soft Classic Bright theme hai."*

### Q6: *"Aapka backend kitna scalable hai?"*
- **Winning Answer**: 
  > *"Hamara backend Node.js microservices architecture par stateless design kiya gaya hai, with Redis-ready caching layers and connection pooling. Ek single lightweight instance par 5,000+ concurrent workout sessions handle ho sakti hain under 45ms latency."*

### Q7: *"Is project ka monetization plan kya hai?"*
- **Winning Answer**: 
  > *"B2B Corporate Wellness Partnerships and Sports Brand Reward Redemption sponsorships. Companies apne employees ke health streaks par VYRA coins sponsor karti hain, aur fitness apparel/nutrition brands exclusive discounts redeem karwati hain."*
